# Caravan Survival v0.12.0

Gold and loot, the cart Workshop, the Cartographer's Office and the region map.

## New

- **Loot and gold:** humanoid enemies drop loot into the caravan's pouch; sell it at any market for silver, or gold for the rare pieces. Breaking an elite shield-wall earns a bounty paid in the next town.
- **Cart modules:** the Workshop sells the next tier of propulsion, bumper, cargo bed and wheels. Tier 3 needs gold. Bigger beds give a 6x8 or 10x12 cart grid.
- **The map:** a MAP button in town opens the Whispering Woods map with towns, routes and danger. The Cartographer's Office sells Surveyed Routes (the whole region) and the Trade Ledger (every town's trade).
- Dire wolves only leap a guard standing in front of the cart.
