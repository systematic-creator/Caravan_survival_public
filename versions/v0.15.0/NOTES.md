# Caravan Survival v0.15.0

Screen fit options in Settings, so the game fills wide phone screens the way you like.

## New

- **Screen fit** (Settings, Options), remembered between launches:
  - **Fit** (default): the whole game at 16:9, centred, with dark bars on wider screens. Nothing is stretched.
  - **Stretch**: fills the whole screen with no bars; on a wide phone everything is drawn a little wider.
  - **Wide**: the road fills the whole width, so you see further ahead and behind; town screens stay 16:9 in the centre.
- On wide phones the town screen no longer sits against the left edge with an empty strip on the right.
- Settings and road event cards now dim the screen behind them and block taps on it.
- The browser build has Fit and Stretch.
- Rain, screen shake and screen fit are saved between launches.
