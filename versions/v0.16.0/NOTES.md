# Caravan Survival v0.16.0

Your game is saved now, and Oakhaven's Town Hall builds lasting upgrades from the goods you haul home.

## New

- **Saving:** the whole run is kept between launches: your town, day, guards, cargo, silver and gold, contracts, standing and loot. The game saves in town, every few seconds, when you switch away and when you set out. Closing it on the road brings you back to the town you left, as you were when you set out.
- **New game** in Settings, Options starts over (it asks for a second tap).
- **Town Hall** (Oakhaven): the Barracks, Alchemical Lab and Training Grounds. Each has two upgrades paid in silver and goods from your cart: iron crates, timber, alchemical fuel or rations.
  - Barracks: Footmen +15% armor; then crossbow bolts fly 10% faster and pierce one more enemy.
  - Alchemical Lab: Footman and Rogue hits coat enemies in acid that strips armor; then Kindling fire spreads 25% wider and burns barricades twice as hard.
  - Training Grounds: Rush drains 15% less stamina; then level 5 skills recharge 12% faster.
- The browser build has all of it (its game is kept in the browser's storage).
