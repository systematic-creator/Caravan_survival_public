# Caravan Survival v0.14.0

Road events with the Corrupted Shrine and Necromancy; contracts now take a deposit and build standing with each town.

## New

- **Road events:** about once a road you reach a landmark and choose. The **Corrupted Shrine** (cleanse it for stamina, or commune with it), an **Abandoned Wagon** (search it for rations, or risk an ambush) and a **Lost Pilgrim** (share food for morale). The road pauses while you decide; a choice you can't afford says why.
- **Necromancy:** Void Communion at the shrine turns an Arcanist to Necromancy for good. Their skill becomes **Soul Drain**: it hurts every enemy around the cart and heals the party.
- **Contract deposit:** taking a contract costs a small deposit (10% of the pay, at least 5 silver), returned on delivery.
- **Standing:** each town's guild remembers you, from -5 to +5. A delivery raises it by 1, a failure drops it by 2, and each point changes that town's contract pay by 5%.
- **Downloads:** each release also has version-free files, so the "latest" links in the README and the version log always get the newest build.

## Downloads
