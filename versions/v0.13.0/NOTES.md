# Caravan Survival v0.13.0

Contracts, bulky loot that takes cart space, and a settings menu with the help guide; the browser build now fills the screen.

## New

- **Contracts:** every Market posts a contract board (3 a week). Carry a sealed crate to another town by its deadline for silver, and gold on dangerous roads. Late or lost, the contract fails. "Where to?" and the map show where contracts are due.
- **Bulky loot:** Rusted Plate and Orc Tusks are cart items now. They need room in the cart (or they're left on the road), add weight, and sell like goods. Small loot still goes into the pouch.
- **Settings:** a gear in the top corner of the town and the road opens Help (the full guide), Options (rain) and About. The road pauses while it's open.
- **Browser build:** fills the screen in landscape with no scrolling page; the guide moved into Settings, Help.
